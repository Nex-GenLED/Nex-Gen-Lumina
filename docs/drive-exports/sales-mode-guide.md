# Sales mode

*Describes build: 2.5.10+116 · Last verified: 2026-10-09 · For dealer sales staff*

Sales mode takes a site visit from first contact to a signed estimate in the app.

## Getting in

Tap the Lumina logo five times on the sign-in screen, or **Installer** under **Nex-Gen Professional Access**, then enter your sales PIN on the **Staff Access** screen. Five wrong attempts lock the pad for 30 seconds. Your session lasts 30 minutes with a five-minute warning.

## The flow

1. **Prospect, step 1 of 3:** customer name, email, phone and address (the address auto-completes), up to six photos of the house, and a referral code if the customer has one. The app says "Code accepted" or "Code not found".
2. **Zones, step 2 of 3:** one zone per run of lights: name, product type, pixels per foot, run length, corners, rail type and colour, connector run, colour preset, notes, and the zone price. Add at least one zone.
3. **Review, step 3 of 3:** proposed Day 1 and Day 2 dates (Day 2 must be after Day 1). **Send estimate to customer** sets the job to estimate sent; **Save draft** keeps it as a draft.
4. **Estimate:** the preview shows one section per zone and the total. **Share with customer** copies a link to the estimate. "What's included" lists the 5-year product warranty, the 1-year labor minimum, the 50,000-hour rated life and ongoing dealer support.
5. **Sign:** the customer signs on the screen under "Approve your estimate". The job becomes signed and appears in the Day 1 Queue.

There is no separate estimate wizard; the three steps above are the whole visit.

## After the signature

- The job waits in the **Day 1 Queue** until the 50 % deposit is marked collected on that queue card, in Installer Mode. A sales session cannot mark it. Agree with your office who does.
- Scheduling, reminders and the install itself are the installer's queues (see the Dealer guide).

## What to say and not say

- Warranty: 5-year product, 1-year labor minimum, 50,000-hour rated life. Never "lifetime warranty".
- Voice: Siri Shortcuts on iPhone and Android app shortcuts. Never promise Alexa or Google Home.
- Away from home: needs the Lumina Bridge, sold and installed by us. A change takes a few seconds.
- Game Day: runs from the customer's phone; they open the app at home in the two days before a game; score celebrations play while the app is open. Do not promise that it runs from our servers.
- The app: the customer gets it by invitation from the installer. It is not in the public app stores.
- Firmware: recorded at install, never updated.
