# Get started with Lumina

*Describes build: 2.5.10+116 · Last verified: 2026-10-09*

Your lights were installed and set up by a professional. This page takes you from the invitation your installer sent to your first look on the house. It takes about five minutes.

## What you need

- The invitation from your installer. On iPhone it opens TestFlight; on Android it opens a Play testing link. Lumina is not in the public app stores, so searching for it will not find it.
- The sign-in email your installer set up for you, and the temporary password they gave you.
- Your phone on your home Wi-Fi.

## 1. Install the app

1. Open the invitation on your phone and follow it to install Lumina.
2. Open Lumina. You'll see the sign-in screen.

**If you don't:** the invitation may have expired. Ask your installer to send it again.

## 2. Sign in

1. Enter the email and temporary password your installer gave you.
2. Tap **ENTER LUMINA**. You'll see the Home screen with a photo of your house.

**If you don't:** tap **Forgot Password?** and enter the same email. A reset link arrives by email. If no account is found, your installer used a different address; ask them which.

## 3. Change your password

1. Tap the **System** tab (bottom right).
2. Tap **My Profile**, then **Security & Password**.
3. Enter the temporary password, choose a new one, and save.

You'll see a confirmation. Your new password works on every phone from now on.

## 4. Allow the two prompts

The first time you open Lumina it asks for two things. Both are optional, but the first one matters:

- Location, "While Using the App". Lumina uses it only to read your Wi-Fi network's name, so it knows when you are at home and can talk to your lights directly. Say yes.
- Local network. Lets the app find your controller on your home Wi-Fi. Say yes.

You can change both later in your phone's Settings.

## 5. Check that your lights are connected

1. On Home, look at the small badge near the top. On your home Wi-Fi it reads **Direct**.
2. Tap the power button. The lights turn on. Tap it again and they turn off.

**If you don't:**

- Home says "No controller is set up yet." Your installer has not finished linking your account. Contact them from **System → Support & Resources → Contact Nex-Gen Support**.
- Home says "Can't reach your lights." Make sure your phone is on your home Wi-Fi, then tap **Reconnect**.
- The badge reads **Via Bridge** while you are standing in the house. Open **System → System & Device Management → Remote Access** and tap **Detect Home Network**.

## 6. Put a look on the house

1. Tap a tile under **My Favorites** on Home, or open the **Explore** tab and tap any look.
2. The lights change within a second or two.

You now know enough to use Lumina every day. The next page covers power, brightness, channels and favorites in more detail.

## The five tabs

| Tab | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Your house, power, brightness, channels, favorites |
| **Schedule** | When the lights turn on and off, and Game Day |
| **Lumina** | Ask for a look in plain words |
| **Explore** | The library of looks |
| **System** | Settings, your profile, support |

## Something is not right?

Open Troubleshooting, or in the app go to **System → Support & Resources** and tap **Contact Nex-Gen Support**. It shows your installer's phone and email. You can also email general@nex-genled.com.

*Facts: T-O8, T-B5, T-R10, T-X19, T-X20, T-SU1, T-SU2, T-S2.*

# Everyday use

*Describes build: 2.5.10+116 · Last verified: 2026-10-09*

Everything on this page happens on the **Home** and **Explore** tabs.

## Power and brightness

- Tap the power button on Home to turn the lights on or off. The button glows when the system is on.
- Drag the brightness slider. The lights follow as you drag.

**If the lights don't follow:** the Now Playing bar reads "Can't reach your lights". Make sure your phone is on your home Wi-Fi and tap **Reconnect**. See Troubleshooting if that does not help.

## Direct or Via Bridge

The small badge near the top of Home tells you how your last command travelled:

- **Direct** — straight to your controller over your home Wi-Fi. Changes are instant.
- **Via Bridge** — through the cloud and the Lumina Bridge at your house. This is how it works away from home. Changes take a few seconds.

Tap the badge to see your recent commands. If it says **Via Bridge** while you are at home, open **System → System & Device Management → Remote Access** and tap **Detect Home Network**.

## Channels

Most homes are wired in more than one run: the front roofline, a garage, a side. Lumina calls each run a channel. The channel chips on Home choose which runs a change goes to.

- Leave every chip selected to change the whole house.
- Tap one chip to change just that run. Tap **All Channels On** to go back to the whole house.
- A chip marked as left out is skipped by looks and designs, but its power switch still works. Tap the chip to include it again.

## Favorites

**My Favorites** on Home holds up to 2 looks you chose. Nothing is added to it on its own.

- **Add one:** in **Explore**, open a look and tap the heart. If you already have 2, the app says "You can keep 2 favorites. Remove one to add another." and offers to replace one.
- **Play one:** tap its tile on Home.
- **Change one:** press and hold a tile, then choose **Replace…** or **Remove from My Favorites**. Removing a favorite does not change your lights.

## Explore

The **Explore** tab is the library. It has nine collections: Architectural Downlighting, Holidays, Game Day Fan Zone, Seasonal Vibes, Parties & Events, Security & Alerts, Movies & Superheroes, Nature & Outdoors, and My Designs.

- Tap any look to put it on the house.
- **Recent Patterns** at the top shows looks you used lately. Each card names the effect it plays. Tapping one plays it again.
- Every effect name you see is the name your controller uses, so what the card says is what plays.

## The design card

Open a look and tap the tune icon to open its design card. You can change the colors, the effect, speed and intensity, and preview on the lights as you go. Nothing is saved until you tap **Save**.

Two controls decide how a multi-color look is laid out when the effect is Static:

- **Static setup**: **Blocks** puts each color in a block along the run; **Alternating** alternates colors light by light.
- **LEDs per color** sets how many lights each color gets. It shows for Alternating and hides for Blocks.

If you preview another effect, the chips stay; tap **Blocks** or **Alternating** to go back to Static.

## My Designs and Design Studio

**My Designs** in Explore holds looks you saved. **Design Studio** (from Home) lets you describe a look in words or paint it light by light on your house photo. Design Studio needs your roofline marked first; if it asks you to mark corners and peaks, tap **Roofline setup** and follow the walkthrough, or ask your installer.

*Facts: T-X20, T-R10, T-V1, T-X22, T-E1, T-E2, T-E3, T-G1.*

# Lumina AI — ask for a look in plain words

*Describes build: 2.5.10+116 · Last verified: 2026-10-09*

Open the **Lumina** tab, or use the Lumina bar on Home. Type or tap the microphone and say what you want.

## Three kinds of request

What you say decides whether Lumina changes the lights now, saves a schedule, or saves a few nights. The words below are the ones that matter.

| You say | What happens |
|---|---|
| "Warm white", "Make it look like Christmas", "Chiefs colors", "Something calm" | The lights change now. Nothing is saved. |
| "…every night", "…nightly", "…every Friday", "…on weekends", or words like "schedule", "turn on at 7", "at sunset" | Lumina offers a repeating schedule and asks you to confirm. It appears in the Schedule tab. |
| "…tonight", "…this evening", "…this weekend", "…this week" | Lumina saves those nights only and tells you so: "They run on those nights only — say 'every night' if you want it to repeat." |

Lumina applies the look to every channel of your house. The reply card says "Sent to: All N channels". If you picked one channel on Home, Lumina still reaches the whole house; use the Schedule tab for a one-channel schedule.

## When Lumina asks a question

- "I couldn't tell what time each night should start. Tell me a time like '7pm' or 'sunset'." Answer with a time.
- "I couldn't work out which nights you meant." Name the days, or say "every night".
- "I couldn't turn that into a design." Name the colors, a team, or a holiday.

## Save a look you like

On the reply card tap **Save as Favorite**. You can keep 2 favorites; the app offers to replace one when you are at the limit.

## Limits

Lumina AI allows 50 requests an hour. If you see "You've reached the hourly AI limit. Try again in an hour.", wait and try again. If you see "Please sign out and sign back in to use Lumina AI.", do that from **System → My Profile → Sign Out**.

## Away from home

Lumina AI works away from home through the Lumina Bridge your installer set up. A look takes a few seconds to land. Schedules and nights you save away from home are stored right away and reach the controller the next time your phone is on your home Wi-Fi.

*Facts: T-L1, T-L2, T-L3, T-L4, T-V1, T-X14, T-R1, T-R8.*

# Scheduling

*Describes build: 2.5.10+116 · Last verified: 2026-10-09*

Schedules live on the **Schedule** tab. Your controller runs them by itself, with the app closed.

## Two kinds of schedule

- **Repeats weekly** — on the days you pick, every week. This is your everyday lighting.
- **Just this day** — one date only. Use it for a party, a holiday, or a game night.

## Make a repeating schedule

1. On **Schedule**, tap **+**. You'll see **New Schedule**.
2. Leave **Repeats weekly** selected.
3. Under **Turn On**, pick **Time** and set a time, or pick **Solar** for sunset.
4. Under **Turn Off**, do the same, or pick sunrise.
5. Pick **Repeat Days**. Pick at least one.
6. Under **Action**, choose what the lights do: **Run Pattern** (choose a look from Explore or My Designs), **Set Brightness**, or **Turn Off**.
7. Tap **Save**.

You'll see the schedule in the list and the header reads "Schedules synced to controller". The lights follow the schedule from the next on or off time.

**If you don't:** the header reads "Not synced — controller offline". Make sure your phone is on your home Wi-Fi, then tap **Sync**.

## Schedule one night only

1. On **Schedule**, tap **+**, then tap **Just this day**.
2. Pick the day, the on and off times, and the look.
3. Tap **Save**.

You'll see "Saved for" that day. It runs that night and then disappears.

A tip: adding a day from the week view makes a repeating item. To add one night, use **Just this day**.

## Sunrise and sunset

Pick **Solar** instead of a clock time. The controller works out sunrise and sunset for your address and adjusts through the year. Two things to know:

- The app needs your home address. If a schedule shows "Set your home address in Settings for exact sun times", add it under **System → My Profile → Edit Profile**.
- Each controller holds one sunrise schedule and one sunset schedule.

There is also a switch under **System** called **Turn lights off at sunrise daily**. With it on, the controller turns the lights off at sunrise every day, whatever else is running.

## How many schedules

- You can save up to 20 schedules in the app.
- The controller holds 8 timed changes at once. If you see "Schedule saved but couldn't arm — controller timer slots are full (8/8). Delete an old schedule.", delete or pause one.
- A one-night schedule within the next two days also uses one of the 8 slots until it has run.

## Away from home

You can edit schedules from anywhere. The app saves them right away and tells you "Saved. Your schedule will arm the next time you're on your home Wi-Fi." The controller gets the change when your phone is next at home.

## Lumina AI can do this for you

Say "warm white every night at sunset" for a repeating schedule, or "red and green this weekend" for two nights. See Lumina AI.

## Autopilot

Autopilot fills your week with looks for holidays, seasons and your teams. Turn it on from the Lumina card on the Schedule tab. **Suggest** waits for your approval; **Proactive** applies its plan itself. Game Day is covered on the next page.

*Facts: T-D1, T-D2, T-D3, T-L4, T-X21, T-R8, T-X23.*

# Game Day

*Describes build: 2.5.10+116 · Last verified: 2026-10-09*

Game Day puts your team's colors on the house for the game and takes them off afterwards. Score celebrations can flash the house when your team scores. This page says what it does, how to set it up, what to expect, and what not to touch.

## What it does

- Before kickoff, the house changes to your team's look. The lead time is yours to set (30 minutes by default).
- During the game, with the app open, the house celebrates scores.
- After the game, the lights go back to your everyday look.

Game Day runs from your phone. The app writes the game's look and a timed change to your controller while you are at home, and the controller makes the change at kickoff by itself. The banner on the Game Day screen tells you how your home is set: **Game Day runs from this phone** is normal. A few homes in Nex-Gen's own testing read **Game Day runs from our servers**; nothing changes for you if yours does.

## Set up a team

1. On Home, open **Game Day**.
2. Tap **+** and pick your team under **Choose a Team**. Use **Search all teams...** for college teams.
3. On the team's card, turn on **Autopilot**. You'll see the next game listed with its date and time.
4. Choose a look under **Design**, or leave the suggested one.
5. Optional: turn on **Live Scoring** to celebrate scores. The app explains: "Your lights will celebrate your team's scores while the app is open."

You'll see the game on the Schedule tab with a Game Day tag.

**If you don't:** the card reads "No teams match". Try the city or the mascot name.

## Before every game: open the app at home

Open Lumina on your home Wi-Fi at some point in the two days before kickoff. That is when the app sets the controller. You can then put the phone away; the lights change at kickoff on their own.

**If you forget:** the lights do not change for that game. Open the app at home and tap **Light Up Now** on the team card to put the look up by hand.

## During the game

- Score celebrations play only while the Lumina app is open on your phone. Keep it open if you want them.
- Each celebration ends on its own and the house returns to the team look.
- Under **Celebration** on the team card you can pick the effect and its **Length**: **Short**, **Medium** or **Long**. A touchdown plays about 8, 15 or 30 seconds. Every celebration is capped at 60 seconds. An NFL extra point does not celebrate.
- **Alerts** sets how often: **All Events**, **Major Only** or **Clutch Only**.

## After the game

The lights return to your everyday look (your usual ON schedule). On new systems that look is Lumina Blue, the standard everyday color; on older systems it is whatever your installer saved. If a game runs very long, the look is taken down eight hours after it started at the latest.

**If the house is still in team colors an hour after the final:** open the app at home and tap your everyday schedule on the Schedule tab, or turn the lights off and on from Home. Then tell your installer which game it was, from **System → Support & Resources** or by emailing general@nex-genled.com.

## More than one team

Add each team on the Game Day screen. Under **Team Priority**, drag to reorder: when two of your teams play at the same time, the top team lights the house and its score alerts are the ones that fire. **Skip day games** skips games played fully in daylight at your home.

## What not to touch

- **Do not open your controller's own web pages** (the address printed on it) and do not add or change outputs there. Game Day checks that your everyday lighting matches your controller; a change made outside the app can block Game Day for your house and leave it in team colors after a game. Ask your installer for any hardware change.
- If the Game Day screen says "Your everyday lighting settings need repairing", contact your installer from **System → Support & Resources**. The app cannot repair this on its own today.
- Game Day and score celebrations do not run through a voice assistant.

## Refreshing games

Tap **Refresh all schedules** at the bottom of the Game Day screen to pull the latest game times.

# After a power outage

*Describes build: 2.5.10+116 · Last verified: 2026-10-09*

When power comes back, your controller restarts. Two things happen that you should know about, and one step fixes both.

## What you'll see

- **The lights come back on by themselves** at a medium brightness, often in one plain color across the whole roofline. This is the controller's own start-up behavior, not your schedule.
- **Only part of the house responds, or the wrong part.** After a restart the controller forgets that your roofline is split into channels. Until the app puts the split back, a change meant for one run can land on the whole strip, or on the wrong run.

Nothing is lost. Your schedules, favorites, designs and Game Day settings are all still there.

## The one step that fixes it

1. Make sure your phone is on your home Wi-Fi.
2. Open Lumina and wait on the Home screen for about a minute. The badge reads **Direct**.

The app reconnects to the controller and restores your channels. From then on the next scheduled change puts the house back on its routine.

**If you don't want to wait for the schedule:** tap a favorite on Home, or tap the power button off and on.

**If Home says "Can't reach your lights":** the controller may still be starting, or your router may not be back yet. Wait a minute and tap **Reconnect**.

## Away from home when it happens

You can turn the lights off or put a look up through the bridge, but the channel split is only restored when a phone with the app is on the home Wi-Fi. If nobody is home, the house will look slightly off until someone is.

## If the lights stay dark after a long outage

Check the controller's power supply and the outlet it is on. If the controller has power and the house is still dark after you open the app at home, use Troubleshooting or contact your installer from **System → Support & Resources**.

*Facts: T-P1, T-P2, T-X20, T-R1.*

# Troubleshooting

*Describes build: 2.5.10+116 · Last verified: 2026-10-09*

Find your problem below and follow the steps in order. Each ends with what to do if it still is not fixed. Contact for anything else: **System → Support & Resources → Contact Nex-Gen Support**, which shows your installer's phone and email, or email general@nex-genled.com with the date and time.

## My lights are dark

1. Is the controller plugged in and its outlet on? If not, fix that first.
2. Is your phone on your home Wi-Fi? Open Lumina. The badge on Home should read **Direct**.
   - If it reads **Via Bridge** at home: open **System → System & Device Management → Remote Access** and tap **Detect Home Network**.
3. Does the Now Playing bar say "Can't reach your lights"? Tap **Reconnect** and wait a minute.
4. Tap the power button. Do the lights come on?
   - Yes: a schedule turned them off. Check the Schedule tab for an off time, and the **Turn lights off at sunrise daily** switch under System.
   - No: did the power go out recently? See After a power outage.
5. Still dark: contact your installer. Tell them the date, the time, and what Home said.

## The lights look wrong, or only part of the house changed

1. On Home, are all the channel chips selected? Tap **All Channels On**.
2. Is a chip marked as left out? Tap it and choose to include it.
3. Did the power go out recently? The controller forgets the channel split until the app reconnects at home. See After a power outage.
4. Did a schedule or Game Day change one run only? Tap a favorite on Home to put one look on the whole house.
5. Still wrong: contact your installer.

## The app says I'm away from home

The app decides from your Wi-Fi network's name. At home, Home reads **Direct**.

1. Is your phone on Wi-Fi, and on your home network (not a guest network or mobile data)?
2. Did you change your Wi-Fi name or router? Open **System → System & Device Management → Remote Access** and tap **Detect Home Network** while on the new network.
3. Did you say no to the Location prompt? The app needs it to read the network name. In your phone's Settings, allow Location for Lumina "While Using the App".
4. If it still reads **Via Bridge** at home, the app works anyway, just a few seconds slower. Tell your installer.

## Away from home, nothing responds or it is slow

1. "You're away from home. Control from anywhere requires a Lumina Bridge." means your home has no bridge paired. Ask your installer about one.
2. "Couldn't reach your home from here" or "Your Lumina Bridge hasn't checked in": the bridge is unplugged, your home internet is down, or the bridge is in its quiet window. About every seven hours the bridge is quiet for roughly ten minutes. Wait ten minutes and try again.
3. Remote commands normally take a few seconds. Wait before tapping again.
4. "You're away from home and remote access isn't set up for this controller": open **System → System & Device Management → Controllers** and check that your controller shows ACTIVE. If not, tap it and choose **Set as Active**.
5. Still nothing after ten minutes: contact your installer.

## Game Day didn't switch back

1. Wait until an hour after the final. The game decides when the look ends.
2. Open the app at home. On the Schedule tab tap your everyday schedule, or on Home turn the lights off and on.
3. Does the Game Day screen say "Your everyday lighting settings need repairing"? Contact your installer; this cannot be fixed from the app today.
4. Tell your installer which game it was and the time.

## Game Day didn't light the house

1. Did you open the app at home in the two days before kickoff? The app sets the controller then. If not, tap **Light Up Now** on the team card now.
2. On the Game Day screen, does the banner read **Game Day runs from this phone**? If it reads "setup needed", tap it and follow what it says.
3. Is **Autopilot** on for that team, and is the game listed on the card?
4. Still nothing: contact your installer with the date and time.

## Nothing responds on a new phone, or after switching accounts

1. Sign in with the same email as before.
2. Open **System → System & Device Management → Controllers**.
3. Tap your controller and choose **Set as Active**. You'll see it marked ACTIVE.
4. Go back to Home and tap the power button.

On the current build the app does this for you after an account switch; on older builds you may have to do it once by hand.

## Voice isn't available

Siri Shortcuts work on iPhone: open **System → Voice Assistants**, save a look, then tap **Add to Siri**. On Android, long-press the Lumina icon for app shortcuts. Google and Alexa options on that screen do not complete today; they are not available.

## A schedule didn't run

1. On the Schedule tab, is the schedule in the list and is the header "Schedules synced to controller"? If it reads "Not synced", go home and tap **Sync**.
2. Did you edit it away from home? It applies the next time your phone is on the home Wi-Fi.
3. Did you see "controller timer slots are full (8/8)"? Delete or pause an old schedule and tap **Sync**.
4. Is it a sunrise or sunset schedule? Make sure your home address is set under **System → My Profile → Edit Profile**.
5. Do you see "Controller clock isn't set"? Open the app at home and leave it on Home for a minute; the app sets the controller's clock. If the message stays the next day, contact your installer.

## I changed my Wi-Fi password or router

Your controller and bridge need the new network. This is an installer task: contact your installer from **System → Support & Resources**. Do not try to reconfigure the controller through its own web pages; when its network is gone the controller opens its own setup network, and your installer uses that to reconnect it.

## I want to remove a controller

Open **System → System & Device Management → Controllers**, tap the controller and choose to remove it. The app warns that this deletes all saved settings for that controller. Ask your installer before doing this.

# Frequently asked questions

*Describes build: 2.5.10+116 · Last verified: 2026-10-09*

**Do I have to open the app every night?**
No. Schedules run on the controller by itself. The app is only for changes, Game Day set-up and celebrations.

**Does it work when my phone is off or away?**
Schedules and the sunrise-off switch, yes. Changing the lights from away needs the Lumina Bridge your installer set up. Score celebrations need the app open.

**Which voice assistants work?**
Siri Shortcuts work on iPhone today, and Android phones can use app shortcuts from the Lumina icon. Alexa and Google Home are not available.

**How many favorites can I keep?**
Two. The app offers to replace one when you add a third. See Everyday use.

**How many schedules can I have?**
Up to 20 saved. The controller holds 8 timed changes at once; a one-night schedule within the next two days also takes one of them. See Scheduling.

**Why does the app say I'm away from home when I'm not?**
It reads your Wi-Fi network's name. Open **System → System & Device Management → Remote Access** and tap **Detect Home Network**. See Troubleshooting.

**Why did a change away from home take a few seconds, or fail?**
Remote commands travel through the cloud and the bridge and take a few seconds. About every seven hours the bridge is quiet for ten minutes; try again then.

**The power went out and the lights came back on by themselves. Is something wrong?**
No. Open the app at home for a minute and everything returns to normal. See After a power outage.

**What does Game Day do when the game ends?**
The lights go back to your everyday look. If they do not within an hour of the final, see Troubleshooting.

**Can I pick how long a celebration lasts?**
Yes. On the team card, under **Celebration**, choose **Short**, **Medium** or **Long**.

**What is "Lumina Blue"?**
The standard everyday color on new systems (a bright cyan). It is what the house shows when your everyday schedule turns the lights on, unless your installer saved a different look.

**Will my firmware be updated?**
No. Your controller's firmware was recorded at install, and nobody updates it. If anyone offers to flash or update it, say no and contact your installer.

**Can I cut or extend the light strips?**
No. The system is a permanent installation. Contact your installer for any change to a run.

**How do I change my Wi-Fi password or router?**
Contact your installer. The controller and the bridge must be joined to the new network at the house.

**I got a new phone. What do I do?**
Install Lumina from your invitation, sign in with the same email, then check **System → System & Device Management → Controllers** shows your controller as ACTIVE. If not, tap it and choose **Set as Active**.

**Can someone else in my home use the app?**
Yes. Ask your installer to add them; invited members get full control.

**Where do I find my support contact?**
**System → Support & Resources → Contact Nex-Gen Support**. It shows your installer's phone and email, or Nex-Gen LED's if no installer is on your profile. You can always email general@nex-genled.com.

**Is my data private?**
Your home address and Wi-Fi name are stored encrypted. Lumina AI requests go to Nex-Gen's own service and on to the AI provider as text only. You can delete your account under **System → My Profile → Security & Password**.

**Which version do I have?**
Open **System**; the version shows at the bottom. The current test build is 2.5.10 (116).
