# After a power outage

*Describes build: 2.5.10+116 · Last verified: 2026-10-09*

When power comes back, your controller restarts. Two things happen that you should know about, and one step fixes both.

## What you'll see

- **The lights come back on by themselves** at a medium brightness, often in one plain color across the whole roofline. This is the controller's own start-up behavior, not your schedule.
- **Only part of the house responds, or the wrong part.** After a restart the controller forgets that your roofline is split into channels. Until the app puts the split back, a change meant for one run can land on the whole strip, or on the wrong run.

Nothing is lost. Your schedules, favorites, designs and Game Day settings are all still there.

## The one step that fixes it

1. Make sure your phone is on your home Wi-Fi.
2. Open Lumina and wait on the Home screen for about a minute. The badge reads **Direct**.

The app reconnects to the controller and restores your channels. From then on the next scheduled change puts the house back on its routine.

**If you don't want to wait for the schedule:** tap a favorite on Home, or tap the power button off and on.

**If Home says "Can't reach your lights":** the controller may still be starting, or your router may not be back yet. Wait a minute and tap **Reconnect**.

## Away from home when it happens

You can turn the lights off or put a look up through the bridge, but the channel split is only restored when a phone with the app is on the home Wi-Fi. If nobody is home, the house will look slightly off until someone is.

## If the lights stay dark after a long outage

Check the controller's power supply and the outlet it is on. If the controller has power and the house is still dark after you open the app at home, use [Troubleshooting](06-troubleshooting.md) or contact your installer from **System → Support & Resources**.

*Facts: T-P1, T-P2, T-X20, T-R1.*
