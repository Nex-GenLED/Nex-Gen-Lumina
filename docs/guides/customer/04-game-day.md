# Game Day

*Describes build: 2.5.10+116 · Last verified: 2026-10-09*

Game Day puts your team's colors on the house for the game and takes them off afterwards. Score celebrations can flash the house when your team scores. This page says what it does, how to set it up, what to expect, and what not to touch.

## What it does

- Before kickoff, the house changes to your team's look. The lead time is yours to set (30 minutes by default).
- During the game, with the app open, the house celebrates scores.
- After the game, the lights go back to your everyday look.

Game Day runs from your phone. The app writes the game's look and a timed change to your controller while you are at home, and the controller makes the change at kickoff by itself. The banner on the Game Day screen tells you how your home is set: **Game Day runs from this phone** is normal. A few homes in Nex-Gen's own testing read **Game Day runs from our servers**; nothing changes for you if yours does.

## Set up a team

1. On Home, open **Game Day**.
2. Tap **+** and pick your team under **Choose a Team**. Use **Search all teams...** for college teams.
3. On the team's card, turn on **Autopilot**. You'll see the next game listed with its date and time.
4. Choose a look under **Design**, or leave the suggested one.
5. Optional: turn on **Live Scoring** to celebrate scores. The app explains: "Your lights will celebrate your team's scores while the app is open."

You'll see the game on the Schedule tab with a Game Day tag.

**If you don't:** the card reads "No teams match". Try the city or the mascot name.

## Before every game: open the app at home

Open Lumina on your home Wi-Fi at some point in the two days before kickoff. That is when the app sets the controller. You can then put the phone away; the lights change at kickoff on their own.

**If you forget:** the lights do not change for that game. Open the app at home and tap **Light Up Now** on the team card to put the look up by hand.

## During the game

- Score celebrations play only while the Lumina app is open on your phone. Keep it open if you want them.
- Each celebration ends on its own and the house returns to the team look.
- Under **Celebration** on the team card you can pick the effect and its **Length**: **Short**, **Medium** or **Long**. A touchdown plays about 8, 15 or 30 seconds. Every celebration is capped at 60 seconds. An NFL extra point does not celebrate.
- **Alerts** sets how often: **All Events**, **Major Only** or **Clutch Only**.

## After the game

The lights return to your everyday look (your usual ON schedule). On new systems that look is Lumina Blue, the standard everyday color; on older systems it is whatever your installer saved. If a game runs very long, the look is taken down eight hours after it started at the latest.

**If the house is still in team colors an hour after the final:** open the app at home and tap your everyday schedule on the Schedule tab, or turn the lights off and on from Home. Then tell your installer which game it was, from **System → Support & Resources**.

## More than one team

Add each team on the Game Day screen. Under **Team Priority**, drag to reorder: when two of your teams play at the same time, the top team lights the house and its score alerts are the ones that fire. **Skip day games** skips games played fully in daylight at your home.

## What not to touch

- **Do not open your controller's own web pages** (the address printed on it) and do not add or change outputs there. Game Day checks that your everyday lighting matches your controller; a change made outside the app can block Game Day for your house and leave it in team colors after a game. Ask your installer for any hardware change.
- If the Game Day screen says "Your everyday lighting settings need repairing", contact your installer from **System → Support & Resources**. The app cannot repair this on its own today.
- Game Day and score celebrations do not run through a voice assistant.

## Refreshing games

Tap **Refresh all schedules** at the bottom of the Game Day screen to pull the latest game times.
