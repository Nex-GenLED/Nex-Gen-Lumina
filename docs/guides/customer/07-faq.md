# Frequently asked questions

*Describes build: 2.5.10+116 · Last verified: 2026-10-09*

**Do I have to open the app every night?**
No. Schedules run on the controller by itself. The app is only for changes, Game Day set-up and celebrations.

**Does it work when my phone is off or away?**
Schedules and the sunrise-off switch, yes. Changing the lights from away needs the Lumina Bridge your installer set up. Score celebrations need the app open.

**Which voice assistants work?**
Siri Shortcuts work on iPhone today, and Android phones can use app shortcuts from the Lumina icon. Alexa and Google Home are not available.

**How many favorites can I keep?**
Two. The app offers to replace one when you add a third. See [Everyday use](01-everyday-use.md).

**How many schedules can I have?**
Up to 20 saved. The controller holds 8 timed changes at once; a one-night schedule within the next two days also takes one of them. See [Scheduling](03-scheduling.md).

**Why does the app say I'm away from home when I'm not?**
It reads your Wi-Fi network's name. Open **System → System & Device Management → Remote Access** and tap **Detect Home Network**. See [Troubleshooting](06-troubleshooting.md).

**Why did a change away from home take a few seconds, or fail?**
Remote commands travel through the cloud and the bridge and take a few seconds. About every seven hours the bridge is quiet for ten minutes; try again then.

**The power went out and the lights came back on by themselves. Is something wrong?**
No. Open the app at home for a minute and everything returns to normal. See [After a power outage](05-power-outage-and-recovery.md).

**What does Game Day do when the game ends?**
The lights go back to your everyday look. If they do not within an hour of the final, see [Troubleshooting](06-troubleshooting.md).

**Can I pick how long a celebration lasts?**
Yes. On the team card, under **Celebration**, choose **Short**, **Medium** or **Long**.

**What is "Lumina Blue"?**
The standard everyday color on new systems (a bright cyan). It is what the house shows when your everyday schedule turns the lights on, unless your installer saved a different look.

**Will my firmware be updated?**
No. Your controller's firmware was recorded at install, and nobody updates it. If anyone offers to flash or update it, say no and contact your installer.

**Can I cut or extend the light strips?**
No. The system is a permanent installation. Contact your installer for any change to a run.

**How do I change my Wi-Fi password or router?**
Contact your installer. The controller and the bridge must be joined to the new network at the house.

**I got a new phone. What do I do?**
Install Lumina from your invitation, sign in with the same email, then check **System → System & Device Management → Controllers** shows your controller as ACTIVE. If not, tap it and choose **Set as Active**.

**Can someone else in my home use the app?**
Yes. Ask your installer to add them; invited members get full control.

**Where do I find my support contact?**
**System → Support & Resources → Contact Nex-Gen Support**. It shows your installer's phone and email, or Nex-Gen LED's if no installer is on your profile. You can always email general@nex-genled.com.

**Is my data private?**
Your home address and Wi-Fi name are stored encrypted. Lumina AI requests go to Nex-Gen's own service and on to the AI provider as text only. You can delete your account under **System → My Profile → Security & Password**.

**Which version do I have?**
Open **System**; the version shows at the bottom. The current test build is 2.5.10 (116).
