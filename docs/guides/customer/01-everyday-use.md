# Everyday use

*Describes build: 2.5.10+116 · Last verified: 2026-10-09*

Everything on this page happens on the **Home** and **Explore** tabs.

## Power and brightness

- Tap the power button on Home to turn the lights on or off. The button glows when the system is on.
- Drag the brightness slider. The lights follow as you drag.

**If the lights don't follow:** the Now Playing bar reads "Can't reach your lights". Make sure your phone is on your home Wi-Fi and tap **Reconnect**. See [Troubleshooting](06-troubleshooting.md) if that does not help.

## Direct or Via Bridge

The small badge near the top of Home tells you how your last command travelled:

- **Direct** — straight to your controller over your home Wi-Fi. Changes are instant.
- **Via Bridge** — through the cloud and the Lumina Bridge at your house. This is how it works away from home. Changes take a few seconds.

Tap the badge to see your recent commands. If it says **Via Bridge** while you are at home, open **System → System & Device Management → Remote Access** and tap **Detect Home Network**.

## Channels

Most homes are wired in more than one run: the front roofline, a garage, a side. Lumina calls each run a channel. The channel chips on Home choose which runs a change goes to.

- Leave every chip selected to change the whole house.
- Tap one chip to change just that run. Tap **All Channels On** to go back to the whole house.
- A chip marked as left out is skipped by looks and designs, but its power switch still works. Tap the chip to include it again.

## Favorites

**My Favorites** on Home holds up to 2 looks you chose. Nothing is added to it on its own.

- **Add one:** in **Explore**, open a look and tap the heart. If you already have 2, the app says "You can keep 2 favorites. Remove one to add another." and offers to replace one.
- **Play one:** tap its tile on Home.
- **Change one:** press and hold a tile, then choose **Replace…** or **Remove from My Favorites**. Removing a favorite does not change your lights.

## Explore

The **Explore** tab is the library. It has nine collections: Architectural Downlighting, Holidays, Game Day Fan Zone, Seasonal Vibes, Parties & Events, Security & Alerts, Movies & Superheroes, Nature & Outdoors, and My Designs.

- Tap any look to put it on the house.
- **Recent Patterns** at the top shows looks you used lately. Each card names the effect it plays. Tapping one plays it again.
- Every effect name you see is the name your controller uses, so what the card says is what plays.

## The design card

Open a look and tap the tune icon to open its design card. You can change the colors, the effect, speed and intensity, and preview on the lights as you go. Nothing is saved until you tap **Save**.

Two controls decide how a multi-color look is laid out when the effect is Static:

- **Static setup**: **Blocks** puts each color in a block along the run; **Alternating** alternates colors light by light.
- **LEDs per color** sets how many lights each color gets. It shows for Alternating and hides for Blocks.

If you preview another effect, the chips stay; tap **Blocks** or **Alternating** to go back to Static.

## My Designs and Design Studio

**My Designs** in Explore holds looks you saved. **Design Studio** (from Home) lets you describe a look in words or paint it light by light on your house photo. Design Studio needs your roofline marked first; if it asks you to mark corners and peaks, tap **Roofline setup** and follow the walkthrough, or ask your installer.
