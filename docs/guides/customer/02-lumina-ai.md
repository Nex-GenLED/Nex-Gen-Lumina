# Lumina AI — ask for a look in plain words

*Describes build: 2.5.10+116 · Last verified: 2026-10-09*

Open the **Lumina** tab, or use the Lumina bar on Home. Type or tap the microphone and say what you want.

## Three kinds of request

What you say decides whether Lumina changes the lights now, saves a schedule, or saves a few nights. The words below are the ones that matter.

| You say | What happens |
|---|---|
| "Warm white", "Make it look like Christmas", "Chiefs colors", "Something calm" | The lights change now. Nothing is saved. |
| "…every night", "…nightly", "…every Friday", "…on weekends", or words like "schedule", "turn on at 7", "at sunset" | Lumina offers a repeating schedule and asks you to confirm. It appears in the Schedule tab. |
| "…tonight", "…this evening", "…this weekend", "…this week" | Lumina saves those nights only and tells you so: "They run on those nights only — say 'every night' if you want it to repeat." |

Lumina applies the look to every channel of your house. The reply card says "Sent to: All N channels". If you picked one channel on Home, Lumina still reaches the whole house; use the Schedule tab for a one-channel schedule.

## When Lumina asks a question

- "I couldn't tell what time each night should start. Tell me a time like '7pm' or 'sunset'." Answer with a time.
- "I couldn't work out which nights you meant." Name the days, or say "every night".
- "I couldn't turn that into a design." Name the colors, a team, or a holiday.

## Save a look you like

On the reply card tap **Save as Favorite**. You can keep 2 favorites; the app offers to replace one when you are at the limit.

## Limits

Lumina AI allows 50 requests an hour. If you see "You've reached the hourly AI limit. Try again in an hour.", wait and try again. If you see "Please sign out and sign back in to use Lumina AI.", do that from **System → My Profile → Sign Out**.

## Away from home

Lumina AI works away from home through the Lumina Bridge your installer set up. A look takes a few seconds to land. Schedules and nights you save away from home are stored right away and reach the controller the next time your phone is on your home Wi-Fi.
