# Lumina Homeowner Guide

<div class="sub">Everything you need to run your permanent outdoor lighting — plus troubleshooting and answers to the questions we hear most.</div>

**Describes Lumina app version 2.5.10+89.**

---

Welcome to Lumina. Your lights are installed, tuned, and already talking to the app. This guide covers the parts you'll use every day first, then the "why is it doing that?" moments at the end.

**Read Sections 1–5 and you're 90% of the way there.** Everything after that is a feature to pick up whenever you're curious.

### What you'll need

- The **Lumina** app on your phone or tablet (App Store / Google Play)
- The **email and temporary password** your installer gave you
- Your **home Wi-Fi** — the same network your controller is on
- About 10 minutes

## 1. Signing in for the first time

Your Lumina account was created for you at install time.

1. Open the **Lumina** app.
2. Enter the **email** and **temporary password** from your installer.
3. Tap **ENTER LUMINA**.
4. You'll be asked to **set a new password** (at least 8 characters). One-time security step.
5. The app loads your home, your lights, and anything your installer set up for you.

If your installer configured a starting look and schedule, you'll see a short welcome screen confirming your lights are ready, with the option to let **Auto-Pilot** take over your lighting plan. Say yes now or turn it on later — Section 9.

<div class="tip">
<strong>Didn't get credentials?</strong> Tap <strong>Forgot Password?</strong> on the sign-in screen and enter your email — or call your installer, who can resend your account details.
</div>

<div class="note">
<strong>Installing the app during the beta.</strong> Lumina isn't in the public App Store or Google Play yet, so you can't search for it — your installer sends you an invitation link instead (TestFlight on iPhone, a Play testing link on Android). The <strong>User Setup Guide</strong> walks through accepting that invitation and getting the app onto your phone. Start there if the app isn't installed yet, then come back here.
</div>

### Permissions the app asks for

Only two, and only when they're actually needed:

- **Location** — powers *Welcome Home* and lets the app recognize your home Wi-Fi network for remote access. You can tap **Skip for Now** and enable it later.
- **Notifications** — game alerts and schedule reminders.

Neither is required to control your lights.

### Taking the tour

The first time you reach the Home screen, a short guided tour appears. Replay it any time from **System → Take the Tour**.

## 2. A quick map of the app

Five buttons live in the glass dock at the bottom of the screen:

| Tab | What lives there |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Your house photo, power, brightness, favorites, tonight's schedule |
| **Schedule** | Your weekly plan — on/off times, patterns, Autopilot |
| **Lumina** (center) | Your AI assistant. Tap to chat, press and hold to speak |
| **Explore** | The design library — holidays, teams, seasons, whites, and more |
| **System** | Settings, controllers, users, remote access, support |

At the top of the Home screen:

- **Hello, [your name]** — your greeting
- A **router icon** — cyan means your controller is online, grey means it isn't. Tap it to switch controllers if you have more than one
- A **gear icon** — jumps to Settings

<div class="tip">
Tapping the tab you're already on returns you to the top of that section. Handy when you've browsed deep into the design library.
</div>

## 3. Your Home screen, part by part

### The house photo

The big card at the top is your home. When the lights are on, Lumina animates the current colors and effect right on the roofline of the photo, so you can see what's playing without walking outside.

No photo yet? Tap **Add Your Home** in the corner of the card and pick a picture of your house. You can also do this later in **System → My Profile → Edit Profile**.

The sky in the photo shifts through the day — dawn, daylight, dusk, night — so the preview matches what you'd actually see outside right now.

### The Now Playing bar

The frosted bar across the bottom of the photo is your main control strip:

- **NOW PLAYING** shows the design currently running. When the lights are off it reads **Lights Off**.
- A badge like **2/4 CH** appears when you've filtered to specific zones (Section 4).
- The **tune icon** opens the *Adjust Pattern* panel.
- The **power circle** turns everything on or off.
- The **brightness slider** runs along the bottom.

<div class="tip">
If a small bookmark icon sits next to the design name, you've tweaked something that isn't saved yet. Tap the name to save it to <strong>My Designs</strong>.
</div>

### Everything below the photo

- **Design Studio** and **Neighborhood Sync** — two feature buttons
- **Game Day** and **My Designs** — your teams and your saved creations
- **Lumina Suggestions** — timely ideas based on the date, season, and what you've used before. Tap **Apply** to try one
- **My Favorites** — one tap applies a favorite instantly
- **Smart Presets** — quick looks prepared for your system
- **TONIGHT / TODAY** — the next scheduled event. Tap it to edit that schedule

### Status messages you might see

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Syncing…** | The app is refreshing from the controller. Normal. |
| **Last Known State** | The app can't reach the controller right now and is showing the last thing it knew. |
| **We can't find your lights — check your WiFi connection** | No controller is registered or reachable. Tap **Retry**, or **Set Up Controller** for a brand-new system. |

## 4. Everyday controls — power, brightness, and zones

### Turning the lights on and off

Tap the **power circle** in the Now Playing bar. Your lights return to the last design at the last brightness.

### Brightness

Drag the **brightness slider**. Changes apply live — slide until it looks right from the street.

<div class="tip">
Brightness and design are separate. Turning the lights off and back on keeps the same design; it doesn't reset to white.
</div>

### Zones and channels

Most homes are wired in several runs — front eave, garage, back fascia, and so on. Lumina calls these **channels**, or **zones** if your installer named them.

To work with one area at a time:

1. Tap the **tune icon** in the Now Playing bar to open **Adjust Pattern**.
2. Scroll to the channel bar. It reads **All Zones** by default.
3. Tap it to expand, then tap a zone name to select just that zone. The header changes to **2 of 4 Zones** and a **FILTERED** badge appears.
4. Any color, pattern, or brightness change now applies **only to the zones you selected**.
5. Tap **All Zones** (or **All**) to go back to controlling everything together.

Each zone chip also has a small **power icon**. Tap it to turn that one run on or off — perfect for lighting just the front of the house.

<div class="warning">
<strong>If part of your house is dark and you can't get it back:</strong> open the channel bar and tap <strong>All Channels On</strong>. That re-selects every zone <em>and</em> powers each one on. This is the fix for "I turned off the back, and now color changes only affect the front."
</div>

### The Adjust Pattern panel

The same panel gives you live control over what's playing:

- **Speed** — how fast the effect moves
- **Intensity** — how dense or dramatic the effect is
- **Colors** — tap a swatch to change any color in the current design
- **Save As Custom Pattern** — keeps your tweaked version in **My Designs**

## 5. Finding a design you love

Tap **Explore**. Three ways in:

**1. Just search.** Type "Christmas", "Chiefs", "purple", "Halloween". If nothing matches, Lumina offers to either **describe it to Lumina** or **build it in Design Studio** — you're never left at a dead end.

**2. Browse the Design Library.** Categories include:

- **Holidays** — Christmas, Halloween, Easter, July 4th, and more
- **Game Day Fan Zone** — pro and college teams by league
- **Seasonal Vibes** — Autumn Harvest, Spring Pastels, Summer Vibrance, Winter Frost
- **Parties & Events** — Birthday Brights, Elegant Dinner, Baby Shower, Rave / Strobe
- **Architectural Downlighting (White)** — Warm Whites, Cool Whites, Gold Accents, brightness gradients
- **Security & Alerts** — Security Bright, Alert Red, Police Lights, Caution Amber
- **Movies & Superheroes**
- **Nature & Outdoors** — Galaxy & Starlight, Twinkling Stars
- **My Designs** — everything you've saved

**3. Tap a color theme or pre-built pattern** on the Explore home screen for the fastest route.

### Applying a design

Once you pick a theme — your team, a holiday — you land on the design screen where you choose how it plays:

1. **Colors** — the theme's colors, your own **My Colors**, a **Blended** mix, or **Auto Colors**
2. **Effect** — solid, twinkle, chase, fade, fireworks, and dozens more
3. **LEDs per color** — how wide each color band is
4. **Speed** and **Intensity**
5. **Brightness Pattern** (optional) — a taper such as *Gentle Fade*, *Staircase*, or *Soft Pulse* for a downlighting look
6. Tap **Apply**

Your lights change within a second or two, the Now Playing bar updates, and that design runs until you change it or a schedule takes over.

<div class="tip">
Found a keeper? Tap the heart to add it to <strong>My Favorites</strong> so it's one tap from the Home screen.
</div>

## 6. Making it yours — adjusting, saving, designing

### Design Studio

**Home → Design Studio** is where you create instead of browse.

1. Type what you want in plain English: *"Red and green with white accents on the peaks"* or *"Warm white glow on the entire roofline."*
2. Tap **Create Design**.
3. Leave **Preview on lights: ON** to watch it appear outside as you refine.
4. Not quite right? Describe the change and generate again, or open **Manual controls** to place colors yourself.
5. Tap **Apply to Lights** when you're happy, and save it so it's there next time.

**Quick Ideas** on that screen give you one-tap starting points: Warm White, Team Colors, Holiday, Downlighting, Chase Effect.

<div class="note">
Design Studio is at its best when your <strong>roofline setup</strong> is complete — that's what tells the app where your peaks, corners, and runs are. Your installer normally does this. Review it under <strong>System → System Management → Roofline Setup</strong>.
</div>

### My Designs and My Favorites

- **My Designs** holds everything you've created or saved. Reach it from the Home screen or Explore.
- **My Favorites** is your shortlist — the grid right on the Home screen.

Tap any design in My Designs to open it. From there you can:

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Apply to Lights** | Put it on the house right now |
| **Edit** | Reopen it in Design Studio to change it |
| **Rename** | Give it a name you'll recognise later |
| **Duplicate** | Copy it, so you can experiment without losing the original |
| **Delete** | Remove it permanently |

<div class="tip">
<strong>If <em>Edit</em> is greyed out</strong>, that design was composed by Lumina from a description rather than built in the editor — there's no editor session to reopen. Use <strong>Duplicate</strong>, or describe what you want again in Design Studio.
</div>

Any design you save works everywhere else: schedule it, hand it to Lumina, or use it for Game Day.

### Lumina Studio — a pro design for the rest of the yard

Want lighting beyond the roofline? **System → Lumina Studio** sends a quote request to a Nex-Gen design specialist.

1. Choose the area: **Landscape**, **Patio**, **Deck**, or **Pool**
2. Add a photo — camera or gallery
3. Drag virtual fixtures (path lights, bistros, and more) onto the photo where you want them
4. Review and **Request Quote**

## 7. Talking to Lumina (your AI assistant)

The center button in the dock is Lumina.

- **Tap** it to open a chat.
- **Press and hold** it to speak instead of typing.

Ask for what you want in your own words:

- "Set my lights to Chiefs colors"
- "Make it look like Christmas"
- "Something warm and relaxing"
- "Dim to 40%"
- "Turn on warm white at 7pm and off at midnight on weekdays"

Lumina can apply designs, change brightness, create and edit schedules, and suggest ideas. It also learns your preferences over time, so its suggestions sharpen the more you use it.

Quick-start chips on the chat screen: **Warm white**, **Sunset vibes**, **Party mode**, **Calm & cozy**, **Surprise me**, **Game day**.

<div class="tip">
Lumina is often the fastest way to build a schedule — just describe the times and the look, and check the result on the Schedule tab.
</div>

## 8. Schedules — set it and forget it

Tap the **Schedule** tab. This is where your lights learn your routine, so you're not reaching for your phone every evening.

### Creating a schedule

1. Tap **+**.
2. Set the **Turn On** time — a clock time, or **Sunset**.
3. Set the **Turn Off** time — a clock time, or **Sunrise**.
4. Choose **Repeat Days** — individual days, **Weekdays**, or every day.
5. Choose **What should the lights do?**
   - **Run Pattern** — then **Choose a pattern**
   - **Set Brightness** — a plain on at a set level
   - **Turn Off** — an off-only event
6. Tap **Save Schedule**.

### Sending schedules to the controller

Schedules live in two places: in your account, and in the controller itself. That second part is what lets your lights run on time even when your phone is off, away, or dead.

The **Sync** button at the top right pushes them across. It normally happens on its own and the button reads **Synced 5m ago** when it's current. Tap it any time to force it.

| Message | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Schedules synced to controller** | Done. Your lights will run on time. |
| **Schedule saved with warnings** | Saved, but one part didn't take. Tap **Sync** again. |
| **Controller not reachable…** | You're not on your home Wi-Fi. The schedule is saved and syncs next time you're home. |
| **Schedule sync failed** | See *Troubleshooting → A schedule didn't run*. |

<div class="warning">
<strong>Sync from home.</strong> Writing schedules into the controller only works on your home Wi-Fi. Build a schedule while you're out and it saves fine — just open the Schedule tab once when you get home and it syncs itself.
</div>

### Things worth knowing

- **How many schedules you can run is set by your controller, not the app.** Your controller has **8 timer slots**. A schedule with both an on-time and an off-time uses **two** — so about **four** clock-based schedules fills it. The schedule editor shows a live **"N of 8 timer slots used"** meter before you save, so you'll see it coming.
- **Sunset and sunrise schedules are free.** They run from their own dedicated slots and don't count against the 8. If you're tight on slots, anchoring to Sunset/Sunrise instead of a clock time buys you room.
- If schedules overlap, an orange banner offers a **Clean Up** tool. Overlapping schedules make lighting unpredictable, so it's worth clearing.
- A schedule's **off** time turns the lights off even if you manually applied something afterward. When that's coming up soon, the app tells you: *"Heads up — your 'Warm White' schedule turns the lights off at 11:00 PM."*
- You can anchor a schedule to **Sunset** or **Sunrise** instead of a clock time — one of each per system. See below.

### Scheduling only part of the house

If your system has more than one channel, the schedule editor includes a **Channels** picker. Leave it on **All channels** for a normal whole-house schedule, or tap individual channels to scope the schedule to just those — front eave only, signage only, and so on.

<div class="warning">
<strong>Scoping turns the rest off.</strong> While a channel-scoped schedule is running, every channel you did <em>not</em> select turns off for its duration. That's deliberate — it's how "light only the front tonight" works — but it surprises people the first time. The editor shows the same warning above the picker.
</div>

On a single-channel system the picker doesn't appear at all — there's nothing to scope.

### Editing and deleting

Tap any schedule under **Recurring Schedules** to edit it; **Delete Schedule** is inside the editor. You can also tap the **TONIGHT / TODAY** card on the Home screen to jump straight into the schedule coming up next.

### Time format

Prefer 24-hour time? **System → My Profile → Edit Profile → Time Format**. The choice applies everywhere a time appears.

## 9. Autopilot — let Lumina plan your week

Autopilot is the hands-off option. Instead of building schedules yourself, Lumina builds a week of lighting for you — holidays, seasons, game nights, and quiet evenings in between.

On the **Schedule** tab, flip the **Autopilot** switch, then pick how much rope it gets:

| Mode | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Suggest** | Lumina proposes changes and waits for you to tap **Apply** |
| **Proactive** | Lumina builds and applies the plan itself |

- **Generate this week** builds a plan you can review day by day.
- **Lumina Suggestions** proposes individual ideas — tap **Apply** to accept, or **Show fewer** if you'd rather not be asked.
- Tap any day in the week strip to see or change what's planned.

Everything Autopilot creates is a normal schedule. Edit or delete any of it, any time.

## 10. Game Day

**Home → Game Day.**

1. Tap **Add a Team**, pick a sport (Football, Basketball, Baseball), and search for your team.
2. Choose the design you want that team to use, or let Lumina build it from the team's colors.
3. Tap **Light Up Now** any time you want team colors immediately — tailgates, draft parties, watch nights.

<div class="tip">
<strong>You don't need a schedule for Game Day to work.</strong> It used to require at least one everyday schedule before it would fire; that requirement was removed. Add a team, turn Autopilot on, and it runs on its own.
</div>

Turn on **Autopilot** for a team and game days handle themselves. Everything below lives on the team's card, under the Autopilot switch.

### The team card, row by row

| Row | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Live Scoring** | The master on/off for score reactions. Off means the house runs your game-day design but never reacts to plays. |
| **Alerts** | *How often* your lights react — see the table below. |
| **Celebration** | *What* fires when they do — the motion for a score or a win. |
| **Skip day games** | Don't bother when the game is fully in daylight at your location. |
| **Design variety** | Rotate through team designs, or use the same one every game. |
| **Lead time before game** | How early the lights kick in. |
| **Refresh Schedule** | Pull the latest game times. |

**Alerts** offers three sensitivities:

| Setting | What you get |
|---|---|
| **All Events** | Celebrate every scoring play |
| **Major Only** | Only touchdowns, home runs, goals and the like |
| **Clutch Only** | Only late-game and go-ahead moments |

Your lights return to whatever was playing once the celebration finishes.

<div class="note">
Score celebrations run while the Lumina app is open on your phone. Keep the app up during the game — the screen can be off.
</div>

<div class="tip">
<strong>Looking for a "Sports Alerts" screen?</strong> There isn't one any more. Everything it used to do now lives on the team's card in Game Day, so there's one place to set a team up instead of two. If you had teams configured under the old screen, they were carried over for you.
</div>

**Game Day crews** let you share the moment: create a crew, share the invite code, and neighbors' lights celebrate with yours.

## 11. Neighborhood Sync — light up the whole street

Neighborhood Sync lets several homes run one continuous animation, so a wave of color travels down the block instead of each house doing its own thing.

**Home → Neighborhood Sync.**

### Starting a crew

1. Tap **Start a Block Party**.
2. Give your crew a name.
3. Choose whether it's a **Public Group** — public groups can be found by nearby neighbors looking to join.
4. Share the **invite code** by text or social media.

### Joining a crew

Tap **Join the Party** and enter the code a neighbor gave you. Or tap **Discover Nearby Crews** to find public groups near you.

### Setting up your house

Each home tells the crew two things:

- **Your LED count** — how many pixels your system has
- **Your position on the street** — so the animation flows in the right order

The host can drag homes into order under **Homes in Sync Order**. Different systems and different LED counts all work together — Lumina scales the animation to each house.

### Running a show

Pick a pattern and start the sync. Every home in the crew picks it up and plays its slice.

<div class="note">
Everyone in the crew should have the Lumina app open on their phone while a sync show runs — that's how each house receives the cue. Whole-crew automation that works with the app closed is coming in an update.
</div>

### Managing a crew

- **Share Invite Code** / **Regenerate invite code** (invalidates the old one)
- **Leave Group** — rejoin any time with the code
- **Transfer Ownership** — hand the host role to another neighbor
- **Dissolve Crew** — remove the group entirely

## 12. Controlling your lights away from home

On your home Wi-Fi the app talks straight to your controller. Away from home it routes through the cloud — and you turn that on once.

**System → System Management → Remote Access.**

### Step 1 — Save your home network

While you're at home and on your home Wi-Fi, tap **Detect Home Network**. This is how the app knows when to use the fast local path and when to go through the cloud.

<div class="tip">
Reading your Wi-Fi network name requires location permission on both iPhone and Android. If you're asked, allow it — the app uses it only for this, and your network name is encrypted before it's stored.
</div>

### Step 2 — Pick a connection mode

**ESP32 Bridge (recommended).** A small Nex-Gen bridge stays plugged in at your house. Your commands go to the cloud, the bridge picks them up, and passes them to your lights on your home network.

- No port forwarding, no router changes
- Works with any internet provider
- Usually installed and paired by your installer

Tap **Test Bridge** to confirm. You want **Bridge Connected**.

**Webhook (advanced / DIY).** Routes commands to a web address pointing back at your house. Requires Dynamic DNS and port forwarding on your router. The app includes a step-by-step **Setup Guide** if you go this route.

### Step 3 — Turn it on

Flip **Enable Remote Access**. The status card then tells you exactly where you stand:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **On Home Network** | Direct local control. Fastest. |
| **Remote Mode Active** | Commands are going through the cloud relay. |
| **Away from Home** | You're off your network and remote access isn't enabled yet. |
| **Offline** | No internet connection on your phone. |

<div class="note">
Remote commands take a few seconds longer than local ones — usually 5–10 seconds. That's the normal round trip through the cloud. Give it a moment before tapping again.
</div>

## 13. Sharing access with your household

Everyone in the house can have their own login — no shared passwords.

**System → Manage Users.**

1. Tap **Invite Family Member**.
2. Enter their email address and tap **Send Invite**.
3. The app shows a **6-character code**. Send it to them — it expires in **7 days**.

On their phone:

1. Download Lumina and create an account (or sign in).
2. Tap **Join with Code**.
3. Enter the 6-character code.
4. They're in — same lights, same designs, their own login and their own conversation with Lumina.

To remove someone, tap their name and choose **Remove Access**. Pending invitations can be revoked before they're used.

<div class="note">
Everyone you invite gets full control of the lights. Per-person permission levels (view-only, kids' access) are on the roadmap. Today, invite the people you'd hand the remote to.
</div>

## 14. Voice assistants

**System → Voice Assistants** walks you through hands-free control.

### Siri (iPhone)

Built in — no account linking needed.

1. Save the looks you use most as scenes.
2. On the Voice Assistants screen, tap **Add to Siri** next to a scene.
3. Record a short phrase — *"Movie time"*, *"Game day"*, *"Lights off."*
4. Say it any time: **"Hey Siri, game day."**

Short, memorable phrases work best.

### Google Home and Amazon Alexa

The app includes guided setup for both, linking your Lumina account to the assistant so you can say things like **"Alexa, turn on the house lights."**

1. Open the Google Home or Alexa app.
2. Add / enable **Nex-Gen Lumina**.

## 17. Troubleshooting

### The lights won't respond

1. Check the controller has power — look for the green LED on the unit.
2. Confirm your phone is on your **home Wi-Fi**, not cellular or a guest network.
3. Look at the **router icon** at the top of the Home screen. Grey means the app can't reach the controller.
4. Pull down to refresh, or tap **Retry** on the orange banner.
5. Unplug the controller for **10 seconds**, plug it back in, and give it a minute to rejoin Wi-Fi.
6. Still nothing? Restart your router, then the controller.

### "We can't find your lights — check your WiFi connection"

Either no controller is registered to your account, or none is reachable.

- Tap **Retry** first — often it's just a slow network moment.
- Brand-new system that was never registered? Tap **Set Up Controller**.
- Been working and suddenly stopped? Your controller likely dropped off Wi-Fi. Reboot it (unplug 10 seconds). If your Wi-Fi password changed recently, the controller needs to be reconnected — see the FAQ, or call your installer.

### The app says "Last Known State"

The app is showing the last thing it heard from the controller because it can't reach it right now. Your lights may well be running fine. Check your Wi-Fi, or enable **Remote Access** if you're away from home.

### A schedule didn't run

This is almost always the controller's clock. The Schedule tab shows a banner with a **How to fix** button when it detects a problem. Three causes:

**1. The controller's clock was never set.** Nothing fires at all.

> Open the controller in a web browser by typing its IP address (find it in **System → System Management → Controllers**). Go to **Config → WiFi Setup** and confirm it's connected to your network. Then **Config → Time & Macros** and enable **"Get time from NTP server."** The clock syncs within a minute once the controller has internet.

**2. The time zone is wrong.** Schedules fire, but at the wrong hour.

> In the controller's web page: **Config → Time & Macros**, set **Time Zone** to your local zone, save, and reboot if prompted.

**3. The schedule never reached the controller.** Open the Schedule tab on your home Wi-Fi and tap **Sync**. You want **Schedules synced to controller**.

Also check: is the schedule **enabled**, and are the right **Repeat Days** selected?

### The lights turned off on their own

A schedule's **off** time did its job. Schedules win over manual changes — deliberately, so your lights never stay on all night by accident. The app warns you when it's coming: *"Heads up — your 'Warm White' schedule turns the lights off at 11:00 PM."*

To keep a look past that point, edit the schedule's off time or switch that schedule off for the night.

### My design changed back to something else

Something scheduled took over — a recurring schedule, an Autopilot event, or a Game Day event. Check the **TONIGHT / TODAY** card on the Home screen and the Schedule tab to see what's queued.

### Only part of the house is lit

You have a zone filter, or a zone powered off.

1. Open **Adjust Pattern** (the tune icon).
2. Open the channel bar.
3. Tap **All Channels On**.

That re-selects every zone and powers each one on.

### Colors look wrong

If reds and greens are swapped or whites look tinted, the controller's **color order** may not match your strip.

> Open the controller in a browser and set **Color Order** to **RGB** — the Lumina default, and the fix for most color-accuracy issues.

If your whites look orange or washed out, mention it to your installer — there's a gamma setting on the controller that may need to be reasserted.

### Remote control isn't working

1. **System → Remote Access** — is **Enable Remote Access** on?
2. Is your **Home Network** saved? Tap **Detect Home Network** next time you're home.
3. Bridge mode: tap **Test Bridge**. You want **Bridge Connected**. **Bridge Not Responding** means the bridge is unplugged or your home internet is down.
4. Remote commands take 5–10 seconds. Give it a moment.
5. Webhook mode: confirm your Dynamic DNS address is current and your router's port forward still points at the controller.

### A pattern won't apply

- **"No controller connected"** — you're not reaching your lights. See the first entry above.
- **"Failed to apply pattern"** — usually a momentary network hiccup. Try again.
- If only one design misbehaves, try another to confirm, then tell support which one.

### A pattern doesn't look how you expected

- Check your **brightness** — dim settings mute colors.
- Confirm you applied it to the **zone you intended** (no leftover FILTERED badge).
- Some effects are built for longer runs and read differently on short ones.

### Everything is slow or the app feels stuck

Force-close the app and reopen it. If the controller was recently unplugged, give it a full minute to rejoin Wi-Fi before expecting a connection.

### Sending diagnostics to Nex-Gen

**System → Support & Resources → Upload system logs.** This sends controller diagnostics to the Nex-Gen team. Do it before you call if you can — it makes the call much shorter.

## 18. Frequently asked questions

### Getting started

<div class="faqq">Do I need Wi-Fi for my lights to work?</div>

Your lights run their schedule from the controller itself, so they keep turning on and off on time even if your internet goes down. You need Wi-Fi to *change* anything from the app.

<div class="faqq">Does my phone have to stay on for my schedule to run?</div>

No. Once a schedule syncs to the controller it runs on its own. Your phone can be off, away, or dead.

<div class="faqq">Can more than one person control the lights?</div>

Yes — invite them under **System → Manage Users**. Each person gets their own login. Section 13.

<div class="faqq">I got a new phone. What do I do?</div>

Install Lumina and sign in with the same email and password. Designs, favorites, and schedules all come with your account.

<div class="faqq">Can I use the app on a tablet?</div>

Yes, same login. **Simple Mode** is a nice fit for a wall-mounted tablet.

<div class="faqq">I forgot my password.</div>

Tap **Forgot Password?** on the sign-in screen, enter your email, and follow the reset link. To change a password you still know: **System → My Profile → Security**.

### Everyday use

<div class="faqq">How do I light only the front of my house?</div>

Open **Adjust Pattern → channel bar**, then tap the power icon on the zones you want off — or select just the front zone and apply a design to it. Section 4.

<div class="faqq">Can I have different colors on different parts of the house?</div>

Yes. Select one zone, apply a design, then select another zone and apply a different one. Each zone keeps what you gave it.

<div class="faqq">What's the difference between My Favorites and My Designs?</div>

**My Designs** is everything you've created or saved. **My Favorites** is your shortlist of go-to looks, shown right on the Home screen.

<div class="faqq">Why does the design name have a little bookmark icon?</div>

You've adjusted something and it isn't saved. Tap the name to save your version to My Designs.

<div class="faqq">Do I have to use the app every night?</div>

No — that's what schedules and Autopilot are for. Most homeowners set a schedule in the first week and open the app only when they want something special.

### Schedules

<div class="faqq">How many schedules can I have?</div>

Up to **20** recurring schedules.

<div class="faqq">Can my lights follow sunset instead of a clock time?</div>

Yes. Choose **Sunset** as your on-time and **Sunrise** as your off-time, and your lights track the real sky all year — no retiming as the seasons shift. Your installer set your home's coordinates during the install, which is how Lumina knows when dusk falls at your address.

Two limits worth knowing: you can have **one sunrise schedule and one sunset schedule** (any others use clock times), and **offsets aren't available yet** — a sunset schedule fires at sunset, not "20 minutes before." If Lumina says it couldn't set a sunset schedule, your controller's location may not be set; your installer can fix that in a minute.

<div class="faqq">Why did my schedule save but not sync?</div>

Writing schedules into the controller only works on your home Wi-Fi. It syncs automatically next time you open the Schedule tab at home, or tap **Sync**.

<div class="faqq">Two schedules overlap. Which one wins?</div>

Whichever event fires last. Overlapping schedules make lighting unpredictable, which is why the app shows an orange banner with a **Clean Up** tool.

<div class="faqq">Can I skip one night without deleting my schedule?</div>

Yes — switch the schedule off, or just apply what you want manually. Remember the schedule's off time still fires; the app warns you when that's coming.

### Designs and features

<div class="faqq">Can I create a look that isn't in the library?</div>

Two ways: describe it to **Lumina**, or build it in **Design Studio** where you place the colors yourself.

<div class="faqq">Are my team's colors included?</div>

Pro and major college teams are in the **Game Day Fan Zone**. Don't see your team — a local high school, say? Ask Lumina and it can build the colors for you.

<div class="faqq">What does Neighborhood Sync actually do?</div>

It makes several homes run one continuous animation so color flows down the street, each home playing its own slice in order. Section 11.

<div class="faqq">Do my neighbors need the same lights as me?</div>

No. Different LED counts and different systems all work — each home reports its pixel count and Lumina scales the animation.

<div class="faqq">Will score celebrations flash while I'm out?</div>

Score celebrations play while the Lumina app is open on your phone, so keep the app up during the game.

### Hardware

<div class="faqq">Can I cut the light strips?</div>

Yes, at the copper pads marked with cut lines — with the power off first. If you're extending or reconfiguring a run, call your installer; the LED counts in the app need to match the hardware.

<div class="faqq">How do I reconnect the controller after changing my Wi-Fi password?</div>

The controller needs the new credentials. If you're comfortable doing it yourself: connect to the controller's own Wi-Fi access point (named **Lumina-XXXX**) and update its Wi-Fi settings. Otherwise your installer can do it in a couple of minutes.

<div class="faqq">Should I change anything in Hardware Configuration?</div>

No — those values were dialed in at install, and wrong ones cause dark sections and bad colors. Call your installer first.

<div class="faqq">Do the lights use much electricity?</div>

Very little. Modern pixel LEDs draw a fraction of traditional holiday lighting, and brightness is the biggest factor — running at 60% instead of 100% cuts consumption noticeably and usually looks better from the street anyway.

<div class="faqq">Will my lights survive winter?</div>

Yes. The system is rated for permanent outdoor installation — rain, snow, and heat.

### Privacy and account

<div class="faqq">Why does the app want my location?</div>

Two features use it: **Welcome Home** (lights on when you arrive) and detecting your home Wi-Fi network name for remote access. Both are optional, and your location stays on your phone.

<div class="faqq">Can Nex-Gen see or control my lights?</div>

Support sees only the diagnostics you choose to send with **Upload system logs**. Your installer can view your system to help troubleshoot.

<div class="faqq">How do I delete my account?</div>

**System → Security → Delete Account**, at the bottom under **Danger Zone**. You'll be asked for your password first, then to confirm. This permanently removes your profile, saved designs, schedules, and photos. It cannot be undone.

Three things are worth knowing before you do it:

- **Your lights keep running.** Deleting your account does not turn anything off or wipe the controller. Whatever schedule is already stored on it keeps running until an installer resets the hardware.
- **A bridge that's still plugged in stays claimed.** The bridge remembers its pairing in its own memory, so deleting your account doesn't free it. If someone else is taking over the house, have them factory-reset the bridge from its dashboard — see the *Lumina Bridge Setup* guide.
- **Ask us to finish the job.** A small amount of data outside your profile — neighborhood crew membership, and any voice-assistant links you set up — isn't removed automatically yet. Email support and we'll clear it by hand.
