# Findings — every inventoried document cross-checked against the truth table

Audited at `c283d62` (app 2.5.10+116) on 2026-10-08. Method: five read-only passes (in-app copy; customer guides; installer guides and SOP; dealer and operations guides; bridge, voice, security and marketing documents) plus a keyword sweep of every non-code text file. Each verdict cites a truth-table id (`docs/overhaul-2026-10/01_TRUTH_TABLE.md`) or a code line read at `c283d62`. Claims with neither are marked **unverifiable**, never guessed. Internal engineering audits (`audit/`, `docs/audits/`, `docs/submissions/`, dated design notes) were classified and keyword-swept, not fact-checked line by line; they are dated records, and their disposition is "archive".

Severity: **P1** can damage hardware, lose data, leak a secret, or mislead someone into a risky action. **P2** a step that fails or a feature that does not exist or is not shipped. **P3** stale wording, old stamps, naming drift. Owner-directed removals (Alexa/Google advertising) are counted as P1.
